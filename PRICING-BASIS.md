Rule used: wholesale ~50-60% under the per-plant price of retail multi-packs, so a reseller can
double it (common US wholesale guidance, e.g. airplantcity.com/pages/wholesale-air-plant) and still land inside
the MercadoLibre range. Tiers go by total plants in the order (mix and match), minimum 50.
