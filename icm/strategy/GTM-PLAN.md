## 1. The organic quality play (his thesis, now the center of the plan)
Most MercadoLibre plant sellers run one blurry photo, two lines of text, no story, no website, no proof. The entire plan is built to win on quality, not on ad spend:
- FULL listing pages: real photos (his 4 bulbosa shots + new scale-in-hand and site photos), complete size/price tables, care instructions, packing/shipping promise, the origin story, and the guarantee. Every field ML offers gets filled - attributes, description, specs. ML's own search ranks complete, fast-answered, well-reviewed listings higher, so quality IS the marketing.
- THE WEBSITE AS TRUST ANCHOR: the Tila site (tila preview, already live) is the thing competitors don't have. The ML description points to it (ML allows brand sites in descriptions/questions within its rules - to verify exact ML policy on external links; if links get stripped, the brand name "Jardines Indigo / Tila" carries it). A buyer checking the page finds a real project, real prices, real origin - that closes wholesale buyers.
- REPUTATION ENGINE: answer every question within an hour (we manage it), ship on time, ask every buyer for their review. Reputation color is ML's free ranking fuel - green reputation + complete pages outrank paid ads from sloppy sellers.
- FREE STORE PAGE: Mercado Libre gives sellers a free Mercado Shops storefront - set it up as the MX storefront mirror of the site (zero cost).
- B2B DIRECT: the event/corporate-gift pitch lane costs nothing but sends.
Phase 2 = US wholesale lots, also organic (direct outreach to US plant shops), once MX proves sales.

## 5. Pricing ladder - proposal vs his 42/60/85 draft
Assumption to confirm with him: 42/60/85 is PESOS per plant. Proposal:
- Base mayoreo (50-199 plants): $42 / $60 / $85 MXN - his numbers, kept. Wholesale-vs-retail spread is healthy: a reseller buying medianas at $60 sells at $245 on ML = ~4x before fees.
- 200-499 plants: 10% off = $38 / $54 / $76
- 500+ plants: 20% off = $34 / $48 / $68
- Floor: do not go below $30 MXN/plant on any size (keeps us above the US wholesale rate in pesos and protects the "cultivated, not wild-cheap" story).
ML takes ~13-16% of the sale; on the $2,100 chica lot that's ~$300-340 MXN, leaving ~$1,760-1,800 before shipping/packaging. Packaging estimate: $15-25 MXN per lot to verify with real material costs.
